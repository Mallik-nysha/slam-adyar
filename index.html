Only at Slam Adyar

Things you won't find at other SLAM gyms.

Every SLAM has the gym floor. Adyar adds sports and disciplines you can switch to when the treadmill gets boring.

Climb

Rock climbing wall

An indoor wall that works your grip, back and legs, and makes you think about every move. Beginners start low with a trainer beside them.

Play

Snooker and billiards

A full table for a frame after your workout, or a slower evening with friends.

Play

Pickleball

Quick to learn and hard to put down. Good cardio that doesn't feel like cardio.

Fight

MMA

Striking, grappling and conditioning with a coach. Builds fitness and confidence at your own pace.

Rest

Yoga Nidra

Guided deep rest. Lie still, follow the voice, and leave lighter than you came in.

Swing

Gada

Training with the traditional Indian mace. Swings that build grip, shoulders and core.

Park

Ather charging point

Ride an Ather? Plug it in when you arrive and leave with a charged scooter.
